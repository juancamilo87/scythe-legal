Sip Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026 · Applies to Sip for Wear OS

Sip is a Wear OS water-reminder app. It schedules local reminders on your watch so you can drink, skip, or snooze. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information.

Who we are

Sip is published by Scythe (developer listed on the Sip Google Play listing). For privacy questions, contact us using the email shown on that listing.

No accounts, no cloud sync

  • Sip does not require an account.
  • We do not operate a server that stores your schedules or reminder history.
  • Schedules, reminder interval, and related settings are stored only on your watch.

Information on your device

Depending on how you use the app, Sip may store on your watch:

  • Reminder schedules you create (days, active hours, on/off)
  • Your chosen reminder interval
  • App preferences (for example debug/test options in development builds)
  • System alarm and notification state needed to fire reminders

This information stays on your device. Sip does not upload your schedules or reminder activity to Scythe servers.

Permissions

Sip may request:

  • Notifications — to show reminder alerts you can act on (Drank, Skip, Snooze)
  • Exact alarms / alarm clock — to schedule reminders at the intended times
  • Vibrate — to get your attention when a reminder fires
  • Full-screen intent (where available) — to present the reminder UI more reliably
  • Run at startup — to reschedule reminders after the watch reboots
  • Foreground service — while an active reminder is alerting

These permissions are used only to run reminders you configured. Sip does not use them for advertising or unrelated tracking.

What we do not collect

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use advertising networks or ad trackers in Sip.
  • We do not include analytics or crash-reporting SDKs that send your schedule data to us.
  • Sip does not use your precise GPS location.
  • Google Play may process install, purchase, and abuse-related data under Google’s own policies when you use Play.

Children

Sip is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Do not use the app if you are under that age.

Security

Reminder data is stored in the app’s private storage on your watch. Keep your device locked. No security practice is perfect.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of Sip after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Use the support or contact email on the Sip page in Google Play.
