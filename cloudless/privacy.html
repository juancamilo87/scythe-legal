Cloudless Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026 · Applies to Cloudless for Android

Cloudless is an offline messaging app. It is designed so your chats stay on your phones and are not uploaded to our servers. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information.

Who we are

Cloudless is published by the developer listed on the Cloudless Google Play listing. For privacy questions, contact us using the email shown on that listing.

No accounts, no cloud chat storage

  • Cloudless does not require an account.
  • We do not operate a chat server that stores your messages or media.
  • Text, voice notes, and video notes are sent directly between devices (for example over Bluetooth).
  • Chat history is stored on your device and is removed when a chat ends or expires, according to the app’s session rules.
  • While a chat is still within its time limit, you can reopen it and reconnect to the same peer over Bluetooth without a Cloudless server.

Information on your device

Depending on how you use the app, Cloudless may store on your device:

  • A display name you choose
  • Cryptographic keys and session data needed to chat securely and reconnect
  • Messages and related chat metadata for active or recent sessions
  • Voice notes and short video notes you record or receive in a chat
  • App settings

This information stays on your device unless you choose to share it with a peer through the app (for example your display name, or text or media you send). We do not upload your chat content to Cloudless servers.

Voice and video notes

You can record short voice notes and video notes in a chat. Recording happens only when you hold the in-app control to capture a note. Captured audio and video are stored on your device as part of the chat, encrypted like other chat content, and may be sent to your peer over the same offline link. They are not uploaded to a Cloudless backend. Media is cleared with the chat when the session ends or expires, according to the app’s session rules.

Permissions

Cloudless may request:

  • Bluetooth / Nearby devices — to discover nearby phones, reconnect after a drop, and exchange messages and media offline
  • Camera — to scan QR codes for joining a chat or safety verification, and to record short in-chat video notes
  • Microphone — to record voice notes and the audio for video notes
  • Notifications — to alert you about messages or an active connection

We do not use the camera or microphone for continuous background recording, advertising, or unrelated cloud upload. Camera and microphone access is used for the features above while you use the app.

What we do not collect

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use advertising networks or ad trackers in Cloudless.
  • We do not include analytics or crash-reporting SDKs that send your chat content to us.
  • We do not use your precise GPS location for chat; nearby discovery uses Bluetooth, not a Cloudless location server.
  • Google Play may process install, purchase, and abuse-related data under Google’s own policies when you use Play.

Children

Cloudless is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Do not use the app if you are under that age.

Security

Conversations (including voice and video notes) are encrypted between participating devices. Chat data on your device is stored with encryption. No security practice is perfect; keep your phone locked and only chat with people you intend to connect with.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of Cloudless after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Use the support or contact email on the Cloudless page in Google Play.
